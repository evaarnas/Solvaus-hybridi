Privacy Policy

For Solvaus-hybridi (Android package: com.gmail.solvaus.hybridi)
Effective date: October 24, 2026

Solvaus-hybridi is a local cube simulator and solver. This policy describes how the Android app handles information.

Information the app processes

The app processes cube states, scramble text, moves, and solver results on your device so it can display and solve a cube. The app does not send this information to the developer or to an app-operated server.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or app-operated crash reporting. It does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or messages.

Permissions and network access

The Android app declares the Internet permission for its WebView and application runtime. The app's functionality uses local app resources and communication with its native solver; it does not use this permission to upload cube data or personal information. The app does not intentionally contact analytics, advertising, or other data-collection services.

Storage and retention

Cube state and scramble information are used while you interact with the app and are not kept in an account or synchronized to a server. On Android, the app extracts bundled Gullicube solver lookup tables into its private app storage when needed. These files are solver resources, not personal information. They remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Android device backup behavior is controlled by your device and account settings.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, or disclose personal information to third parties. The Android operating system and Google Play may process information under their own terms and privacy policies when you install or use the app or enable device backup.

Security

Cube solving is performed on your device. App files are kept in Android's app-private storage. No method of storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so keep your device and operating system protected.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when those changes take effect. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
